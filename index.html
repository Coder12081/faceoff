FACEOFF is a fast, easy-to-learn card game for 2 or 4 players. Pass cards you don't need, collect four-of-a-kind sets, and race to empty your hand first. Keep your cards secret, think ahead, and watch what comes your way.

The rules

How to play

The goal is simple: collect all four cards of a rank, discard the set, and be the first player with no cards left.

1

Prepare the cards

Take all four Queens, Jacks, Kings, and Aces from a standard deck. You will have 16 cards in total.

2

Deal them evenly

For 2 players, deal 8 cards to each player. For 4 players, deal 4 cards to each player. Keep your cards hidden.

3

Pass and collect

At the same time, every player chooses one card they don't need and passes it face down to the player on their left. Pick up the card you receive and repeat.

The aim

Four matching cards. One winner.

Whenever you have all four cards of the same rank, place that set face up in a discard pile. For example, four Queens make a set. Keep passing and collecting until someone has no cards remaining.

Quick reference

  • Players: 2 or 4
  • Cards: 16 total: four Jacks, four Queens, four Kings, and four Aces
  • Passing: Everyone passes one unwanted card face down to the left at the same time
  • Sets: Discard all four cards of the same rank together
  • Winner: The first player to have no cards remaining wins immediately
